Our Story

We Want to Help You Achieve a Better Tomorrow

Tuple Academy was built for every student who was told "you need experience to get experience." We believe that ends today.

Welcome To Tuple Academy

Preparing Today's Students for Professional Jobs

We are a skill development and internship platform with one mission — to prepare India's college students for the real professional world. We know that knowledge alone isn't enough in today's competitive landscape.

That's why every internship at Tuple Academy is built around real tasks, real feedback, and real deliverables — not just videos and quizzes. When you complete a Tuple internship, you have something concrete to show: GitHub projects, design files, reports, and a QR-verified certificate.

We started with the belief that a student from a tier-3 college in Chhattisgarh deserves the same career opportunities as one from IIT Delhi. That belief drives everything we build.
